Deepcore strands you on a silent mining world with a drill, a jetpack, and a rocket that still needs building. Beneath the camp, four layers of earth conceal the minerals and exotic materials that can buy your passage home.

Every expedition starts with a shaft. Cut downward, follow a promising vein, and fill your hold—but watch the weight. A loaded jetpack climbs slowly, and an overloaded one cannot lift at all. The richest haul is worth nothing if you burn your last fuel before reaching daylight.

Back at camp, sell your ore, pay for fuel and repairs, and decide what matters next: a stronger drill, a larger tank, a scanner, emergency supplies, or another piece of the rocket. Your tunnels remain, making each return trip part of a growing network beneath the surface.

Deeper down, pale gas seeps and molten seams turn a careless cut into a crisis. The final ignition material lies at the planet’s Core, and extracting it starts a ninety-second countdown. Plan your route home before you make that last cut.
